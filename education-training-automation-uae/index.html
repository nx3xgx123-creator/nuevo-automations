The problem

How training centres lose enrolments

Course enquiries are a comparison exercise. A parent messages three tutoring centres on a Sunday evening asking about maths for Grade 5; an adult messages two language schools about IELTS preparation after work. The centre that answers clearly, with the right group, time and fee, and offers a trial class, is the one they visit. Centres that reply the next afternoon are already out of the running.

The enquiry that doesn't convert straight away is the other gap. "Let me discuss with my husband" or "we'll decide before term starts" is a real maybe, and most centres never follow it up because the admin team is busy with current students. Enrolment periods make it worse: the enquiries peak at the same time as the admin work.

The full journey

Automating the enrolment journey, step by step

  1. Enquiry: answered in seconds, in the language the parent or student writes in. Parents in Arabic and students in English both get replies in their own language.
  2. Qualification: the assistant asks the age or grade, subject or course, preferred branch or online, and timing, and scores the enquiry hot, warm or cold.
  3. Trial or placement test: booked into your timetable with a confirmation and a reminder before it.
  4. After the trial: a message asking how it went, and a follow-up for families who haven't enrolled.
  5. Before term: a final nudge to undecided families, and a broadcast about the new term to your list.

Enquiries from your website or ad forms can be sent to OrcaFlo through Zapier or the API, so the assistant starts the WhatsApp conversation for you during business hours. Every enquiry can also sync to HubSpot, Zoho CRM or a Google Sheet, so your admissions team sees the full pipeline (more on our CRM automation page).

Beyond enquiries

Custom automation for training centres

Once enquiries are handled, the admin around existing students is the next place automation helps. These we build as Custom Automation on the tools you already use:

  • Fee reminders: a WhatsApp reminder before each term's fee is due, and a polite escalation if it's overdue.
  • Class change notices: a cancelled or moved class announced to that group's parents in one step.
  • Attendance follow-up: a message to parents when a student misses classes, so it's noticed early.
  • Certificates and reports: course completion documents generated from a sheet and sent out at the end of term.

OrcaFlo connects to the WhatsApp Business number you already use, in one of two ways. Meta's official WhatsApp Business API gives you the verified route; Meta bills its own per-message fees to your account, and messages sent more than 24 hours after a customer's last message need a Meta-approved template. A QR link to the WhatsApp Business app is quicker to set up and has no Meta fees, but it carries no green tick and some ban risk, because WhatsApp doesn't officially allow unofficial apps. We'll recommend one on the call. OrcaFlo's price is the same on both.
