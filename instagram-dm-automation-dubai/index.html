The problem

Why Instagram enquiries go cold

In Dubai, a lot of buying starts on Instagram. Someone sees a reel, comments "price?", and messages two or three other businesses at the same time. The one that answers first, in the customer's language, usually gets the sale. Meanwhile your team is replying to DMs one by one between everything else, and comments pile up under posts where nobody follows them up.

Instagram automation fixes the speed problem without making your account feel robotic. The assistant answers with your real prices and details, asks the questions your team would ask, and passes serious buyers to a person.

How it works

The rules Instagram automation follows

Instagram automation runs inside Meta's rules, and it's worth knowing them before you start:

  • Professional account needed. You need an Instagram professional account (Business or Creator). It connects with your Instagram login; no Facebook Page is needed.
  • 24-hour window. Replies go out within 24 hours of a customer's last message.
  • One private reply per comment. Meta allows one private reply per comment, within 7 days of the comment.
  • No Instagram broadcasts. The assistant replies to people who comment or message you; it doesn't send broadcasts on Instagram. For offers to a list, use WhatsApp broadcasts.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I auto-reply to Instagram comments with a DM?

Connect your Instagram professional account to OrcaFlo, then add a comment rule: every post and reel or only some, for every comment or only words like "price". Each commenter gets a reply under the comment and a private message.

Do I need a Facebook Page?

No. Instagram connects with your Instagram login. You need an Instagram professional account (Business or Creator).

Can it send broadcasts on Instagram?

No. It replies to people who comment or message you, within 24 hours of their last message. For offers to a list, use WhatsApp broadcasts.

Does it work with Instagram ads?

Yes. Comment rules can cover an ad's post, and people who tap a click-to-Instagram ad are greeted about that offer and asked up to 3 qualifying questions.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes, in Gulf Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic or English, and it switches when the customer does.
