What OrcaFlo actually does

In plain terms: it's an AI that answers your WhatsApp for you, around the clock, and only involves you when someone's ready to buy.

  1. 1

    Someone messages you

    A customer messages your WhatsApp at any hour — 2 AM, a Friday, a public holiday. OrcaFlo replies in under five seconds, in Arabic or English.

  2. 2

    It answers like your best employee

    It's trained on your services, prices and the way you talk. It handles questions, sends photos, listens to voice notes, and books appointments or takes orders.

  3. 3

    You step in only when it matters

    When a lead is hot, it pauses and pings you. Anyone who goes quiet gets chased automatically for a week. Nothing gets forgotten.
