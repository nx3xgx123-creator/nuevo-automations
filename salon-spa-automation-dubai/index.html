The problem

The messages that pile up while your team is busy

Salon messages arrive when nobody can answer them: during appointments, between back-to-back clients, and late at night when someone is scrolling Instagram and decides they want a blow-dry tomorrow. How much is a lash lift? Is anyone free this afternoon? Can I have the same stylist as last time? Do you have a bridal package for five? What's your cancellation policy? Do you do home service?

Each unanswered message is a chair that might stay empty. And the bigger leak is quieter: regular clients who would happily come back for their next colour or refill, but nobody reminded them, so they booked wherever had a slot when they finally thought of it.

Retention

Rebooking: the automation most salons skip

New clients cost you ads and discounts. Regulars only cost a well-timed message. A rebooking flow uses the gap that makes sense for each service and sends a personal nudge when it's due:

  • Hair colour: a reminder a few weeks after the last colour, offering two open slots.
  • Nails and lash refills: a shorter cycle, timed to the refill window you set.
  • Spa and facial courses: the next session in a package, before the client forgets they have one left.
  • No-shows: a friendly follow-up offering a new slot, instead of losing the client.
  • After the visit: a review request on WhatsApp while the result is fresh.

Each follow-up stops as soon as the client replies, so nobody gets chased after they've answered. If your bookings live in a separate salon system with an API, rebooking reminders driven from that system's data can be built as Custom Automation.

Setup

Calendars, team and connection

OrcaFlo books into its own calendar with your working hours and blackout days, or connect Google Calendar so it only offers times you're free and adds each booking there; a Calendly or Cal.com link can be shared in the chat instead. You describe your team and who does what in the assistant's instructions, and it passes preferences on with each booking.

Clients can book at any hour, so a message at midnight becomes a booking for tomorrow. They can send a voice note, which is transcribed, or a photo of a look they like, which the assistant can read; your team can step into any chat to send before-and-after photos themselves.

OrcaFlo connects to the WhatsApp Business number you already use, in one of two ways. Meta's official WhatsApp Business API gives you the verified route; Meta bills its own per-message fees to your account, and messages sent more than 24 hours after a customer's last message need a Meta-approved template. A QR link to the WhatsApp Business app is quicker to set up and has no Meta fees, but it carries no green tick and some ban risk, because WhatsApp doesn't officially allow unofficial apps. We'll recommend one on the call. OrcaFlo's price is the same on both.
