Our Flagship Product

OrcaFlo — The AI Sales System Built for UAE Clinics

OrcaFlo is a fully managed WhatsApp AI platform that handles the entire patient sales cycle for aesthetic, dental, and cosmetic clinics — from first message to booked appointment. Your team only steps in when a lead is hot and ready.

Instant Response

Replies to every WhatsApp enquiry in under 5 seconds, 24/7, in Arabic and English including Khaleeji dialect.

Lead Temperature Scoring

Every lead is automatically tagged cold, warm, or hot based on conversation signals so your team knows who to prioritise.

7-Day Follow-Up Sequences

Leads who go quiet get three personalised follow-ups at 24 hours, 3 days, and 7 days — written by AI based on their actual conversation.

Persistent Memory

The AI remembers every lead across every conversation. No patient ever has to repeat themselves.

Human Takeover

When a lead is ready, the bot pauses, fires a Telegram alert to your team, and lets them reply directly. No app switching.

Broadcast Campaigns

Send promotions to your full lead database with staggered delivery to avoid bans.

Voice & Photo Handling

Patients can send voice notes (transcribed instantly) or photos (read by AI vision). The bot responds accordingly.

CRM Dashboard

Full conversations view, lead list with temperature filters, live chat panel, pause/resume controls, and CSV export.
