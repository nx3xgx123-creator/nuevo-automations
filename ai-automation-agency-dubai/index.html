What an agency does

Why hire an agency instead of buying another tool?

Most Dubai businesses don't lack software. They have a WhatsApp Business number, an Instagram account, a booking system or spreadsheet, maybe a CRM — and a team copying information between them by hand. Buying one more app rarely fixes that. What fixes it is someone who looks at how work actually moves through your business, decides what is worth automating first, wires the pieces together and stays responsible when something changes.

That is the job of an AI automation agency, and it is how we work. We scope the problem with you, build the automation, test it on real conversations and real data, and then keep it running. Fixes and updates are included for as long as you run the system, so you are not left with a clever workflow nobody on your team knows how to repair.

The "AI" part matters most where your customers write in free text. A rule-based bot breaks the moment someone asks something unexpected; an AI assistant trained on your services, prices and brand voice can answer in Arabic or English, understand a voice note, read a photo and still know when to hand the chat to a person.

Example automations

What this looks like day to day

  • An ad lead at 11 PM. Someone fills in your lead form after hours. The lead lands in your CRM, your sales team gets a WhatsApp notification, and the AI assistant has already greeted the customer and asked your qualifying questions.
  • A customer who goes quiet. Instead of the lead being forgotten, OrcaFlo sends three personalised follow-ups written from that lead's actual conversation — not a template blast.
  • A booking tomorrow morning. The customer gets a confirmation, a reminder 24 hours before and another 2 hours before. If they don't show, a follow-up offers a new slot.
  • An invoice nobody chased. Payment reminders go out on WhatsApp on schedule, and overdue accounts are escalated to the right person automatically.
  • The owner's evening check-in. An end-of-day brief lands on your phone with conversation counts, hot leads by name, escalations and what customers kept asking about.
