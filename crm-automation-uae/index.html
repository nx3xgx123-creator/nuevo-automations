Short answer

CRM automation means every new lead is captured into your CRM — HubSpot, Zoho or even a Google Sheet — the right person is alerted on WhatsApp straight away, and follow-ups go out on schedule without anyone remembering to send them. We build it as Custom Automation ($400–10,000 by scope) on the CRM you already use, and pair it with OrcaFlo when leads arrive by WhatsApp or Instagram.

The problem

Where leads leak out of a CRM

Most CRM problems aren't CRM problems. The software is fine; the trouble is everything around it. Leads from a Meta ad form sit in a download nobody imports. WhatsApp enquiries never make it into the CRM at all. A salesperson sees a new lead three hours later, calls once, and the lead is never touched again.

Most leads don't say no — they go quiet, and nobody chases them. Fixing that doesn't need a new CRM. It needs the capture, the alert and the follow-up to happen automatically, every time, so the only thing left for your team is the conversation that closes the sale.

Your CRM

HubSpot, Zoho or Google Sheets

HubSpot

We connect your lead sources to HubSpot contacts and deals, trigger alerts when a deal is created or changes stage, and keep follow-ups moving so nothing sits in a pipeline column for weeks.

Zoho CRM

Same idea, built on Zoho: leads in from every source, assignment rules, WhatsApp alerts to the owner of the lead, and reminders when a lead hasn't been touched.

Google Sheets

Plenty of UAE businesses run sales from a spreadsheet, and that's fine. We can make a Google Sheet behave like a lightweight CRM: new rows from forms and ads, alerts on new leads, and status-based reminders.

No CRM at all?

If most of your leads come through WhatsApp, OrcaFlo includes a CRM dashboard: full conversations, a lead list with cold/warm/hot filters, live chat, pause/resume controls and CSV export. Every lead is scored automatically and followed up at 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days.

Examples

Example CRM automations

  • Meta lead form → HubSpot → WhatsApp: the lead becomes a contact and deal, the salesperson gets a WhatsApp alert, and a follow-up sequence starts if the deal doesn't move.
  • WhatsApp enquiry → CRM: leads qualified by OrcaFlo are recorded with their temperature, so your CRM reflects the conversations happening on WhatsApp.
  • Stale lead reminder: if a lead hasn't been contacted within your target time, the owner and the manager are both pinged.
  • Weekly pipeline summary: new leads, deals won and leads waiting, sent to the owner every Monday.

Many of these workflows run on n8n, Make or Zapier — see workflow automation with n8n, Make and Zapier.
