The problem

Where UAE online stores lose orders

Customers find you on Instagram, TikTok or your website, then message on WhatsApp: is it in stock, do you have it in M, how much is delivery to Al Ain, can I pay cash. Every slow answer is an order going to a faster store. Late evening is the busiest time for these questions and the quietest for your team.

Then there's the admin. Someone copies the name and address into a spreadsheet, sends the bank details or a payment link by hand, checks whether it was paid, and messages the courier. Cash-on-delivery orders get lost between chats. Customers who asked a price and disappeared never hear from you again. Each step is small; together they decide how many enquiries become paid orders.

Store-side automation

Abandoned carts, restock lists and order updates

Some of the most useful e-commerce automations start in your store, not in a chat. These we build as Custom Automation, usually with n8n, Make or Zapier connected to your store platform:

  • Abandoned cart reminders: when a checkout is started but not finished, a WhatsApp reminder with what was in the cart, sent once and not repeated. On the official API, a message like this uses a Meta-approved template.
  • Abandoned chats: separate from carts, OrcaFlo follows up people who asked about a product in WhatsApp and went quiet, and stops the moment they reply.
  • Restock lists: customers who ask for a sold-out size or product are added to a list for that item. When it's back, one broadcast goes to that list only, instead of the whole customer base.
  • Order status updates: confirmations, dispatch and delivery updates on WhatsApp, triggered from your store or courier data.
  • COD reconciliation: a daily sheet of cash-on-delivery orders by status, so nothing is lost between the chat and the courier.
  • Returns and complaints: routed straight to a person, with the order details attached.

Channels & hand-over

Instagram, hand-over and connection

The same assistant can answer your Instagram DMs and reply to comments with a private message, so a "price?" under a post becomes a conversation (see Instagram DM automation). Customers can send a photo of a product they saw, which the assistant can read, or a voice note, which is transcribed.

A complaint, a return or refund, a wholesale or very large order, or a question your product list doesn't answer is handed to your team: the assistant pauses for that customer, your staff get a push alert, and they reply from the shared inbox.

OrcaFlo connects to the WhatsApp Business number you already use, in one of two ways. Meta's official WhatsApp Business API gives you the verified route; Meta bills its own per-message fees to your account, and messages sent more than 24 hours after a customer's last message need a Meta-approved template. A QR link to the WhatsApp Business app is quicker to set up and has no Meta fees, but it carries no green tick and some ban risk, because WhatsApp doesn't officially allow unofficial apps. We'll recommend one on the call. OrcaFlo's price is the same on both.

FAQ

Common questions

Can customers pay inside the WhatsApp chat?

Yes. Once we connect your Strabl or Ziina account, the assistant sends a payment link with the right amount and the order is marked paid automatically when the payment comes through.

Does it handle cash on delivery?

Yes. If the customer prefers to pay at the door, the assistant logs a cash-on-delivery order with their details for your team.

Can it send abandoned cart reminders?

Abandoned checkout reminders start in your store, so we build them as Custom Automation connected to your store platform. Follow-ups to people who asked in WhatsApp and went quiet are built into OrcaFlo.

How does a restock list work?

Customers who ask for a sold-out item are added to a list for it. When it's back in stock, one broadcast goes to that list only, paced to protect your number.

How many products can it know?

You upload your product list and documents in the dashboard and edit them any time; it answers from them and won't invent a price.

Does it answer Instagram DMs too?

Yes. The same assistant answers Instagram DMs and replies to comments with a private message, in the same inbox as WhatsApp.
