The problem

Why WhatsApp needs automating in the UAE

In the UAE, WhatsApp is where buying conversations happen. Customers message several businesses at once, often late in the evening, and the business that replies first in the customer's language usually wins. A third of enquiries land when nobody is working; by the morning, the person who messaged at 9 PM has often booked elsewhere.

Manual replies also break down during the day. Your team answers the same price and availability questions over and over, forgets to chase the leads who said "let me think about it", and never gets round to messaging past customers about new stock or offers. None of that needs a person. It needs a system that does the repetitive part and hands the conversation to your team when a lead is ready.

How it connects

Official API or QR link: which connection?

OrcaFlo connects to the WhatsApp Business number you already use, so there is no new number to announce. There are two ways to do it, and we'll recommend one on the call.

Meta's official WhatsApp Business API

The official route. You keep the WhatsApp Business app on the same number, so your team can still read and reply on the phone. Meta charges its own per-message fees, billed by Meta to your own account, and messages sent more than 24 hours after a customer's last message need a Meta-approved template. The connection itself carries no ban risk, because it is Meta's own platform. OrcaFlo's guide to the WhatsApp Business API in the UAE lists Meta's current fees.

QR link to the WhatsApp Business app

The quick route: scan a QR code and the assistant starts answering on your existing app. There are no Meta per-message fees, but WhatsApp doesn't officially allow unofficial apps, so there is some risk. We'll walk you through the trade-off for your volume and how you use WhatsApp.

OrcaFlo's price is the same on both connections.
