The problem

Why a front desk alone can't keep up

Clinics, salons and service businesses live on bookings, and most bookings now start with a WhatsApp message. The questions repeat all day: how much is it, who is available, do you have anything this evening, where are you. Each one interrupts a receptionist who is also checking people in and answering the phone.

Then the day ends. A third of enquiries land when nobody's working, and customers comparing options message three or four businesses at once. The one that replies first usually gets the booking. A full-time front desk hire runs $1,100–1,650 a month and still only covers eight hours. An AI receptionist covers the other sixteen, and takes the repetitive questions off your team during the day.

What it does

What the AI receptionist handles

Answers instantly

Under 5 seconds, 24/7, in Arabic (including Khaleeji dialect) and English, from your price list, services and timings.

Books appointments

Offers available times and confirms the booking. With Google Calendar connected, it only offers times you're free.

Sends reminders

Confirmation, then reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before the visit — fewer no-shows without a call list.

Reschedules & recovers

Rescheduling over WhatsApp, and a follow-up to customers who didn't show to offer a new slot.

Requests reviews

After the visit, a personalised review request goes out on WhatsApp automatically.

Hands over to staff

Anything that needs a person is paused and your team gets a push alert to reply from the shared inbox.

By business type

How it works for your kind of business

Clinics and medical centres

Patients ask about consultation fees, doctors, timings and directions. The AI receptionist answers those from your information and books the appointment. It handles the front desk, not medicine: symptoms, results and anything clinical are handed to your staff with the whole conversation. OrcaFlo has a dedicated page on its WhatsApp receptionist for clinics.

Salons, spas and beauty

Clients want prices and the next free slot, often from a photo of a look they like. The assistant can read photos and voice notes, quote your price list and book the chair, even while your team is with clients.

Service businesses

For home services, studios, gyms and professional services, the receptionist qualifies the enquiry, asks the questions your team would ask, scores the lead cold, warm or hot, and books a visit or call. Leads who go quiet get follow-ups at 24 hours, 3 days and 7 days.

Example

A typical evening with an AI receptionist

  • 9:40 PM: a customer asks, in Arabic, how much a treatment costs and whether there's anything tomorrow evening. The receptionist answers with your price and offers two free times.
  • 9:41 PM: the customer picks one. The booking is confirmed and added to your calendar.
  • Next day, 2 hours before: a reminder goes out. The customer asks to move it; the receptionist offers new times and reschedules.
  • After the visit: a personalised review request is sent on WhatsApp.

Your team didn't touch any of it — and the owner brief that evening shows the booking, plus any questions customers kept asking.

FAQ

Common questions

Can an AI receptionist book real appointments?

Yes. It offers available times and confirms the booking. With Google Calendar connected it only offers times you're free and adds each booking there; your staff see every booking in the dashboard.

Will it give medical advice to patients?

No. It handles front-desk questions like fees, timings and directions. Anything clinical is handed to your staff with the full conversation.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes, in Arabic including Khaleeji dialect, and in English.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. It connects to your existing WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official API or a QR link to the WhatsApp Business app.

Can my staff take over a conversation?

Yes. The assistant pauses, your team gets a push alert from the OrcaFlo app, and they reply directly from the shared inbox.
